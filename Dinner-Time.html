Dinner Time

A short film about a man, that the universe seemingly hates, just trying to get back in time for dinner.

Type
Short Film
Genre
Comedy, Sci-Fi
Status
In Production
Run Time
~20 Minutes

Harrod, an overworked, overtired husband has one job - get back in time for dinner with a bag of carrots. However, getting back in time for dinner is never as easy as it seems.

A comedic, action-packed sci-fi short that is able to deliver on both comedy, and impressive visual effects work. Using experimental VFX technology to help pull off the ambitious story whilst still remaining on an indie budget.
